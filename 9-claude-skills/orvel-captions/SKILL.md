# Orvel captions (skill 4 of 5)

This skill writes captions for:
- reels, taking the beat sheet from **orvel-reel-script**
- grid posts and carousels, taking the copy from **orvel-instagram-posts**

The account is @orvelsound. The link in bio is orvelsound.com.

## Claim rules (same as every Orvel skill)

- **Mechanism:** acoustic masking only ("a steady floor makes the same noise a smaller jump").
- **Banned:** entrainment, brainwaves, deep sleep, better or faster sleep, medical conditions, minute counts.
- **No invented proof:** no testimonials, stars, customer numbers or "most popular". The $99 tier is "Recommended".
- **Driver captions:** always include "For rest stops only. Never while driving."
- **Facts:**
  - Sleep $69 (2 tracks), Full Day $99 (4), Complete $149 (5). One-time, no subscription required.
  - About 20 questions. Tracks built in 24–48h. MP3 + WAV.
  - One free rebuild within 14 days, and a full refund if the rebuild still misses.

## Voice

- Plain and specific, with short sentences.
- Use real shift-work details: school run, bin lorry, reefer hum, 3 × 12 roster, 06:00 clock-out.
- No hype words, no emojis in body text, no "we're excited to".
- → and ↓ arrows are fine for the CTA.

## Structure

Put a blank line between parts.

1. **Hook line.** Restate the headline or the reel's hook. It must work in the first 125 characters, before "…more".
2. **Body.** 1–2 short paragraphs. Keep list items (tracks, prices, steps) on consecutive single lines.
3. **Action line** (optional): save, tag, swipe or comment.
   - "Save this for your next day sleep."
   - "Tag a nurse coming off nights."
   - "Swipe for the tracks and pricing."
   - "What wakes you first? Tell us below."
4. **Link line:** `Link in bio → orvelsound.com`. Use a niche page only once Advay confirms it's live.
5. **Hashtags:** 4–5 in total, always ending with #orvelsound.

**Length:**
- Grid posts: 250–600 characters.
- Carousels: up to 900 characters.
- Reels: 150–400 characters. The voiceover already carries the story, so the caption adds the hook, one line of value and the CTA.

## Alt text

Write one plain sentence per image, for accessibility. Describe the visual and the headline text. Example: "Cream graphic with the headline 'Three twelves. Then sleep at 9am while the street wakes up' and an example sleep brief."

## Output format

```
POST/REEL: <NN_slug>   Niche: <...>   Type: <grid | carousel | reel>

CAPTION:
<caption exactly as it should be typed>

CHARACTERS: <n>   FIRST LINE UNDER 125: yes/no
ALT TEXT: <one line per image>
CLAIM CHECK: <one line>
```

For a batch, write everything into one `CAPTIONS.txt`, in posting order, with a `====` header per post.
