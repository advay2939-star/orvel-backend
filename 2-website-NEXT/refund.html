Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Free revisions

Every track includes one free revision within 14 days of delivery. Tell us what's off and we'll rebuild it.

Refunds

If your track still isn't right after that revision, we'll refund you in full. Email us within 14 days of delivery.

Before production starts

If you change your mind before we've begun building, email us and we'll refund you in full.

Orvel Refresh (optional add-on)

Orvel Refresh is optional and unticked by default at checkout. If you add it, the first 30 days are free and you are then charged $15 per month until you cancel. Nothing is charged during the free period, and you can cancel at any time — before or after it ends — by emailing orvelsound@gmail.com. Cancelling stops all future charges immediately. Your purchased tracks are unaffected and remain yours.

How to request

Email orvelsound@gmail.com with your name and payment reference.
