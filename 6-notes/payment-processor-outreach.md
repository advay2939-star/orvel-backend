I've signed up and I'm told someone will be in touch. Before that call, here is the

## 5. Razorpay appeal

**How to send:** raise a ticket from the dashboard under Account & Settings — a
dashboard ticket is attached to your merchant record, a cold email to a support alias
isn't. If you have a relationship contact from the beseen.skin approval, email them
the same text separately.

**Subject:** Website review appeal, orvelsound.com: this is a commissioned service, not audio track sales

Hi,

My edit request to add orvelsound.com was rejected on the ground that businesses
operating in the sale of audio tracks fall outside supported categories. I'd like to
appeal that, because the classification doesn't match what the business does, and I'd
rather explain it than resubmit into the same queue and get the same result.

Orvel is not a music or audio track seller. There is no catalogue and nothing is sold
off a shelf. A customer fills in a questionnaire about their work schedule, what
disrupts their sleep, and the kind of sound they find comfortable. A producer then
builds a set of audio files specifically for that person and delivers them once, as a
download, normally within 24–48 hours. Each order involves production work and exists
only because someone commissioned it.

The distinction matters for your risk assessment, and I think it is why the automated
classification is wrong:

There is no third-party copyright or royalty exposure. Nothing licensed is resold. The
work is produced here.

There is no resale or redistribution. Each delivery is one file set for one buyer,
made to their brief.

The chargeback profile is not a media-download profile. The customer specifies the
product before it exists, and there is a defined revision and refund process — one
free revision within 14 days of delivery, a full refund if the revision still misses,
and a full refund if they change their mind before production starts. Buyer's remorse
disputes on stock media happen because the buyer never engaged before paying. That is
not the shape of this transaction.

For completeness: there is one optional add-on at checkout, unticked by default, free
for 30 days and then $15/month, cancellable at any time by email. The cancellation
route is stated on the refund page. It is opt-in and nothing recurs without it.

I already hold an approved website on this account, beseen.skin, with no disputes or
compliance issues on it.

If the category call is final I accept that and I'll go elsewhere. But I'd like it
reviewed by someone who can look at the actual product rather than the automated
classification. Could you route this to an account manager, or tell me who I should be
speaking to?

Happy to walk anyone through the site and the order flow on a call.

Thanks,
Advay
